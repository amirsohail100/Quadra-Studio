Inspect Live Projects

Step 03

Place Your Custom Order

Select your preferred service tier or submit custom requirements directly.

  • Select Package: Choose Machine Learning ($150), Web App ($300), or Gen AI.
  • Fill Requirements: Provide project specifications and your registered Email ID.
  • Get Order ID: After submitting, save your unique Order ID for payment.

Step 04

Verify & Pay Securely

Use our secure payment portal to verify your order details and complete payment.

  • Strict Verification: Enter registered Email ID and Order ID to fetch client name & amount.
  • Scan UPI QR: Pay instantly using GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.
  • PNB Bank Transfer: Direct bank details available for NEFT / IMPS transfers.
